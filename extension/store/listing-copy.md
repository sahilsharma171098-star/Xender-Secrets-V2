## Long description

Check the current webpage for SEO basics, accessibility issues, usability problems and conversion friction — directly in your browser.

Click the SiteCheck icon on any website and, in about a second, you get:

• A Website Health Score (0–100) with separate scores for SEO, Accessibility, Usability, Conversion and Technical basics
• Issues grouped into Critical, Warnings and Recommendations
• For every issue: the problem, why it matters, and the recommended fix — in plain English
• Examples of the exact elements involved, so you or your developer can find them quickly

WHAT IT CHECKS (47 checks)
• SEO basics: page title, meta description, canonical URL, noindex, Open Graph tags for social sharing, H1 headings
• Accessibility: images without alt text, unlabeled form fields, buttons and links without names, heading order, invalid ARIA, page language and measured low-contrast text
• Usability: links that go nowhere, javascript: links, malformed URLs, forms without a submit button, missing autofill hints
• Conversion: missing or vague calls to action, too many competing calls to action, no visible contact method, missing trust pages, long forms. These are clearly labelled as heuristics, not failures
• Technical and security basics: mobile viewport, doctype, HTTPS, mixed content, insecure form actions, passwords on HTTP pages, oversized images, very large pages

BROKEN LINK CHECK
Click "Check links" to verify up to 40 links on the same website. A link is only reported as broken when the server actually returns an error (404, 410 or 5xx). Links that time out are shown as "could not be verified", never as broken.

SHARE THE RESULTS
"Copy report" puts a plain-text report on your clipboard to send to your developer or agency.

HONEST BY DESIGN
• The score is a documented Xender SiteCheck heuristic score. It is not a Lighthouse or Core Web Vitals score.
• This is a quick automated check, not a complete WCAG accessibility audit.
• Contrast is only measured where it can be measured reliably.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Runs only when you click it, and only on the current tab.
• The page is analyzed locally in your browser. Audit results are never sent to Xender Secrets or anyone else.
• No browsing history, form values, passwords, cookies or personal data are collected. There are no analytics, no ads and no remote code.
• Only two permissions: activeTab and scripting.

WANT A HUMAN REVIEW?
SiteCheck is made by Xender Secrets, a small web studio in India. If you'd like a person to look at your site, you can request a free website audit at xendersecrets.com. This is completely optional; the extension never asks for your details.
