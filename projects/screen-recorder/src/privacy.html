Recording privacy

Screen, microphone and webcam capture begins only after you click Start and approve your browser's permissions. The video and timestamp notes stay in this recorder tab's memory until you download them or close the tab. Downloads are saved to your device.

No recordings or notes are uploaded. There is no account, analytics, advertising or third-party tracking. Pausing pauses the saved recording; camera and microphone streams remain active until Stop.

Keep this tab open and download before closing. A crash or reload can lose unsaved recordings. At approximately 250 MiB of video data the recorder stops to limit memory use. Firefox captures screen/window video and optional microphone audio; it does not capture system/tab audio.
