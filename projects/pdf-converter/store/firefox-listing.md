# Xender PDF Converter - Firefox listing

Name: Xender PDF Converter — Offline PDF Tools

Summary: Convert JPG, PNG and WebP to PDF, export PDF pages as images, merge PDFs, extract pages and rotate documents. Free local tools. No file uploads or account required.

Description:

Your PDFs. Sorted.

Xender PDF Converter puts five practical document tools in a Firefox tab. Click the toolbar icon, choose your files, run a tool and download the result. Your files are processed locally on your device.

• Images to PDF: combine JPG, PNG or WebP images, reorder them, and choose A4, US Letter or image-sized pages.
• PDF to images: save selected pages as PNG or JPG. A single page downloads as one image; multiple pages download in a ZIP.
• Merge PDFs: combine multiple PDFs in the order you choose.
• Extract pages: save selected pages in a new PDF, including a custom page order.
• Rotate PDF: rotate all or selected pages by 90, 180 or 270 degrees.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No files are uploaded. All conversion code and rendering libraries are included in the add-on. No account, analytics, third-party advertising or browsing-history access. Works offline after installation. Selected files and outputs are held in the extension tab's memory; clear files or close the tab to release them.

FREE TOOLS, OPTIONAL CUSTOM WORK
The conversion tools are free. A separate, clearly labelled optional link lets businesses request a quote from Xender Secrets for paid custom PDF automation or document tools. It opens our website only when clicked. Your documents and their names are never included. No payment is needed to use any tool in this version.

HONEST LIMITS
For ordinary unencrypted PDFs. Password-protected PDFs, OCR and Word/Excel conversion are not supported. Up to 30 input files / 80 MB combined. PDF-to-images exports up to 50 pages per run, with a 16-megapixel limit per image. PDF editing may remove interactive features or invalidate signatures. Your originals are never overwritten.
