WEBSITE PLANNING

How to Plan a Website Project Before Development Starts

A checklist for goals, pages, content, functionality, domain, analytics and launch planning.

Xender Secrets Editorial · Published 4 October 2026 · Practical guide

Define the outcome

A website can generate enquiries, sell products, explain a service, publish content or support existing customers. Choose the primary outcome first because it shapes navigation and page priorities.

List pages and user journeys

Write down the pages you actually need and what a visitor should do on each one. A simple service site may need Home, Services, About, Contact and policy pages. An ecommerce store needs additional product, cart and checkout flows.

Prepare real content early

Development moves faster when business information, product details, photos and policies are available before the final build. Placeholder text is useful for layout but should not become permanent by accident.

Separate launch essentials from later features

Identify what must work on day one and what can be added later. This keeps the first release focused and reduces the risk of delaying launch for low-priority ideas.

Plan ownership and maintenance

Decide who controls the domain, repository, hosting account, analytics and business email. Keep credentials out of source code and document how updates are deployed.
