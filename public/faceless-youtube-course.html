Lesson 1

Niche aur pehle 10 topics

Faceless channel me aapka chehra nahi, aapki jaankari hi brand hai. Isliye aisa niche chuno jahan aap har video me kuch naya samjha sako: koi hisaab, koi comparison, koi "andar ki baat".

Hamara example: chhote Indian businesses ka hisaab. Chai stall, momos, dhaba, kirana: ek din me kitna bikta hai, kharcha kitna hai, aur maalik ke haath me kya bachta hai.

Topics kaise dhoondhe (free)

  • YouTube search bar: "chai business" type karo aur neeche aane wale suggestions likh lo. Yahi woh shabd hain jo log sach me search karte hain.
  • Milte-julte channels: unki videos "Popular" se sort karo. Topic copy mat karo, bas samjho ki logon ko kya pasand aa raha hai.
  • Comments: logon ke sawaal agli video ke topic hain.

In topics se door raho

  • Stock market, crypto, loan ya "jaldi ameer bano" tips
  • Doctor, vakeel ya CA jaisi pakki salah (AI voice ko expert bana ke pesh karna YouTube ke rules me risky hai)
  • Asli private logon ka naam lekar unhe judge karna
  • Politics aur dharm

Kaam: apne niche ke 10 topics ek list me likh lo. Agle lesson me inme se pehle topic ki script banegi.

Lesson 2

AI se script likhna (free)

Script ke liye Google AI Studio (Gemini) ya ChatGPT ka free version kaafi hai. Asli fark prompt se padta hai. Neeche wala prompt copy karo aur [ ] wali jagah apna topic daalo.

Prompt 1 · Short (~50 second) 
Tum ek Hindi YouTube Shorts script writer ho.
Topic: [chai stall ka ek din ka hisaab]
Length: 45–55 second (lagbhag 130–150 shabd), Hindi (Devanagari) me.
Format: 8 scenes. Har scene ke liye do cheezein do:
1) narration: bolne wali line
2) picture: is line ke saath kaunsi photo dikhe (India ka context, ₹ wale note)

Rules:
- Pehli line 12 shabdon se chhoti ho: koi number, chaunkane wali baat ya sawaal.
- Hisaab step by step, round numbers me (jaise ₹10 × 200 cup = ₹2,000).
- Shuru me ek baar saaf bolo ki ye aankde sirf udaharan ke liye anuman hain.
- Maalik ki apni mehnat aur dheeme din bhi gino, sirf achhe din nahi.
- Har 2–3 scenes me ek nayi baat khule, taaki log end tak dekhein.
- End me ek sawaal poochho jiska jawab log comment me dein.
- Koi investment, loan ya "jaldi ameer bano" wali baat nahi.

Script aane ke baad usi chat me ye doosra prompt bhejo. AI khud apna kaam check karega:

Prompt 2 · Editor check 
Ab ek strict editor ban ke is script ko check karo:
1) 1 se 10 tak score do: hook, samajh me aana, aur hisaab sahi hai ya nahi.
2) Har woh line batao jo galat, zyada pakki, ya check karne layak lagti hai.
3) Phir sudhra hua poora version do.

Score 7 se kam ho toh dobara likhwao. Aur numbers ek baar khud jaanch lo: AI kabhi-kabhi galat hisaab bhi poore bharose se bol deta hai.

Long video (10–13 minute) ke liye

Prompt 1 me ye badlo: "45–60 scenes, lagbhag 13 minute. Har ~50 second pe ek naya chapter: ek item ki keemat, saman ka kharcha, roz ke fixed kharche, sab bik gaya wala din, dheema din, break-even, maalik ki mehnat, daam badalna, cash flow, hisaab rakhna, aakhri seekh."

Lesson 3

Free Hindi AI voice

Tarika A: bina kuch install kiye

Clipchamp (Microsoft ka free video editor, browser me chalta hai) me text-to-speech hai. Script paste karo, Hindi voice chuno, aur voice seedhe aapki video me aa jaati hai. Agle lesson ki editing bhi wahin ho jaayegi.

Tarika B: laptop pe, automation ke liye

Hamara channel edge-tts use karta hai. Ye ek free Python tool hai jo Microsoft ki natural Hindi voices se audio banata hai. Script ko script.txt me save karo aur ye chalao:

Terminal 
pip install edge-tts
edge-tts --voice hi-IN-SwaraNeural --rate=+5% -f script.txt --write-media voice.mp3 --write-subtitles voice.srt
  • hi-IN-SwaraNeural female voice hai, hi-IN-MadhurNeural male.
  • voice.srt me har shabd ka time aata hai. Isi se captions bante hain.
  • Ye Microsoft ki official paid service nahi hai, isliye kabhi-kabhi band ya slow ho sakti hai. Backup ke liye Tarika A yaad rakho.

Zaroori: AI voice wali video upload karte waqt YouTube ko batana hota hai. Lesson 7 me iska tarika hai.

Lesson 4

Free pictures aur "hisaab cards"

Har scene ke liye ek picture chahiye. Pixabay pe free photos, illustrations aur videos milte hain jo YouTube pe use kiye ja sakte hain. Download se pehle unka license ek baar padh lo: brand logo ya pehchaane jaane wale logon wali photos me dhyan rakhna padta hai.

Sahi picture kaise chune

  • Picture wahi dikhaye jo us waqt bola ja raha hai. "200 cup chai" bola toh chai ke cups, sunset nahi.
  • India ka context rakho. Hamari ek test video me rupaye ki baat pe dollar ke note aa gaye the. Viewers turant pakad lete hain, isliye har picture ek baar dekh lo.
  • Short me har 5–7 second pe picture badlo. Isse video chalti hui lagti hai.

Hisaab cards: sabse kaam ki cheez

Jab hisaab bola ja raha ho, tab picture ki jagah ek saada text card dikhao, jaise:

Card ka example 
Roz ki bikri
₹10 × 200 cup = ₹2,000
Doodh, cheeni, gas = – ₹1,100
Bacha = ₹900

Ye cards Clipchamp ya Canva ke text tool se 2 minute me ban jaate hain. Viewer ko hisaab samajh aata hai, aur aapki video bhi "sirf stock footage" nahi lagti.

Lesson 5

Free editing (bina watermark)

Clipchamp ka free plan 1080p tak bina watermark export karta hai aur usme AI subtitles bhi free hain. Isliye shuruaat ke liye yahi kaafi hai.

  1. Size chuno: Short ke liye 9:16 (1080×1920), long video ke liye 16:9 (1920×1080).
  2. Pehle voice daalo. Poori video voice ke hisaab se chalti hai.
  3. Har line ke neeche uski picture ya hisaab card rakho.
  4. Captions on karo (auto subtitles). Bahut log bina awaaz ke dekhte hain.
  5. Upar ek chhota headline text do, jaise "₹2,000 roz?"
  6. Music optional hai: YouTube Studio ki Audio Library se free track lo aur volume kam (~20%) rakho, taaki voice dabe nahi.
  7. Export 1080p me karo.

Short 3 minute se chhota hona chahiye. Hum ~50 second rakhte hain.

Lesson 6

Title, thumbnail aur description

  • Title: jo shabd log search karte hain, woh shuru me rakho. Jaise: "Chai stall business: ek din ki kamai ka poora hisaab".
  • Thumbnail (long video): 1280×720, ek saaf picture aur 3–4 bade shabd. Custom thumbnail ke liye channel ko phone se verify karna padta hai (youtube.com/verify).
  • Description: pehli 2 lines me video ka saar. Long video me chapters (00:00 Intro, 00:48 Kharcha…), picture credits aur ek line ki AI voice use hui hai.

Links ka dhyan: Shorts ki description aur comments me diye link click nahi hote. Shorts me "link channel profile me hai" likho, aur clickable link long videos ki description me ya channel profile ke links me rakho.

Lesson 7

Upload settings aur channel safe rakhna

SettingKya chunna hai
Altered or synthetic contentYes, agar AI voice ya realistic AI visuals hain. Hum har video pe yahi karte hain.
Made for kidsNo (jab tak video sach me bacchon ke liye na ho)
CategoryApne niche ke hisaab se (hamari: Education)
Kitni videosRoz 1–3. Naye channel pe bahut zyada uploads spam jaise lagte hain.

"Inauthentic content" rule samjho

15 July 2025 se YouTube "mass-produced" aur ek jaisi baar-baar repeat hone wali videos ko monetization se bahar kar sakta hai. AI use karna mana nahi hai. Problem tab hoti hai jab har video ek hi template ki copy lagti hai aur usme aapka koi apna yogdaan nahi dikhta.

  • Har video me kuch apna do: apna hisaab, apna angle, apni seekh.
  • Styles badalte raho: kabhi cost breakdown, kabhi myth vs fact, kabhi comparison.
  • Doosron ki videos thoda badal ke dobara upload mat karo.
  • Fake views, likes ya "sub4sub" kabhi mat karo. YouTube channel band kar sakta hai.

Lesson 8 · Level 2

Automation: video khud bane aur khud upload ho

Lessons 1–7 haath se karne par hi channel chal sakta hai. Lekin hamara channel roz apne aap chalta hai, aur ye bhi ₹0 me. Ye level thoda technical hai (Python aur GitHub ki basic samajh chahiye). Yahan dekho ki poora system kaise juda hai:

  1. Schedule: GitHub Actions roz tay time pe kaam shuru karta hai (hamare liye din me 2 baar).
  2. Research: YouTube Data API se niche ki trending videos, search suggestions aur apni purani videos ke views dekhta hai.
  3. Script: Gemini API likhta hai, phir "editor" step score deta hai. 7 se kam score ho toh dobara likhwata hai.
  4. Voice: edge-tts Hindi voice aur har shabd ka timing banata hai.
  5. Pictures: Pixabay API se har scene ke liye kai options aate hain, aur Gemini dekh ke woh chunta hai jo line se sach me match kare.
  6. Video: FFmpeg pictures, voice, captions aur hisaab cards jod ke final video banata hai.
  7. Upload: YouTube Data API upload karta hai, AI disclosure lagata hai, playlist me daalta hai aur pehla comment karta hai.

Shuru karne se pehle ye jaan lo

  • API audit: naye Google Cloud project se API ke through upload hui videos tab tak private rehti hain jab tak project YouTube ka audit pass na kare. Audit form free hai, par approval me time lagta hai.
  • Limits: free tiers ki daily limits hain, aur YouTube naye channels ke uploads pe bhi limit lagata hai. Roz 1–3 videos hi sahi hain.
  • Nazar rakhna: hafte me ek baar channel khol ke dekh lo. Galat fact wali video dikhe toh hata do.

Lesson 9

Monetization ka sach

YouTube se ads ki kamai ke liye channel ko YouTube Partner Program (YPP) me aana padta hai. Abhi ke rules:

Abhi (21 Oct 2026)1 Feb 2027 se naye channels
Subscribers1,0001,000
Long videos ka raasta4,000 watch hours (12 mahine)8,000 watch hours (12 mahine)
Ya Shorts ka raasta1 crore Shorts views (90 din)2 crore Shorts views (90 din)

Ek chhota tier bhi hai (500 subscribers, 90 din me 3 uploads, aur 3,000 watch hours ya 30 lakh Shorts views) jisme memberships jaisi fan-funding milti hai, ads ki kamai nahi. Ye har desh me available nahi hai, isliye YouTube Studio ke Earn tab me apna status dekh lo.

  • Long videos zaroori hain: watch hours unhi se aate hain. Shorts se log aate hain, long video unhe rokti hai. Har Short ko "related video" se apni long video se jodo.
  • Doosre raaste: affiliate links, apni service ya product, aur aage chal ke sponsorships.
  • Sabr rakho: zyaadatar naye channels ko mahino lagte hain. Jo log 3 mahine lagatar achhi videos daalte hain, woh hi aage nikalte hain.

Sources: YouTube Blog: YPP updates (2027) · YouTube Help: YPP overview

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Kya ye course sach me free hai?

Haan. Saare 9 lessons free hain, koi signup ya payment nahi.

Kya chehra dikhana zaroori hai?

Nahi. Is course ki har video AI voice, pictures aur text cards se banti hai.

Kya sirf phone se ho sakta hai?

Lessons 1–7 phone se bhi ho sakte hain (Gemini/ChatGPT app aur Canva app). Clipchamp browser me laptop pe zyada aaram se chalta hai. Lesson 8 ki automation ke liye laptop chahiye.

Kya AI wali videos monetize hoti hain?

Ho sakti hain, agar har video me asli value ho aur woh ek jaisi copy-paste na lage. Mass-produced, repeat hone wali videos ko YouTube monetization se bahar kar sakta hai (Lesson 7).

Ek video banane me kitna time lagta hai?

Shuru me haath se ek Short me 1–2 ghante lagte hain. Practice ke saath ye kam hota hai, aur automation (Lesson 8) ke baad roz ka kaam bas ek nazar daalna reh jaata hai.
