# Xender PDF Converter privacy policy

Updated 17 October 2026. Applies to Chrome version 1.0.1.

Files you select are processed locally in the extension for images-to-PDF conversion, PDF-to-image export and PDF page operations. Selected files can contain personal or other sensitive information. They are used only to perform the operation you requested and are not uploaded to Xender Secrets or a conversion service.

The extension has no account, analytics, third-party advertising, trackers, remote scripts, cloud storage or browsing-history access. It does not save file contents or names in browser storage. All processing libraries and decoders are bundled with the extension.

Selected files and outputs are held temporarily in the extension tab's memory. Clear files or close the tab to release them. User-initiated downloads save output to your device; you control those downloads and can delete them with your file manager. The extension cannot remove a downloaded copy after saving.

The optional paid-service quote is a separate business enquiry on the Xender Secrets website. It does not unlock features or charge for local conversion. Clicking it sends no file contents, file names or document metadata. Information you choose to enter on the website is governed by that website's privacy practices.

There is no sale or sharing of document data, no use for unrelated advertising/profiling, and no creditworthiness or lending use. Support: https://www.xendersecrets.com/contact . Do not send private documents through support.
