# Website development sales / outreach SOP

## Research → qualify → draft → approve → log → deliver

1. Choose one country, city and vertical; start with accountants, clinics, gyms, restaurants or service firms with an observable contact/booking problem. Directory discovery is manual or through authorized APIs. No scraping behind login, CAPTCHA circumvention, purchased lists or guessed email addresses.
2. Save exact listing URL, business-owned contact URL, observed-at timestamp and factual problem. Verify the actual business website and current contact details. A directory entry is discovery evidence, not consent. Deduplicate by normalized business domain and contact address; retain original lead ID. Example Business LEAD-001 is a fixture and must never be contacted.
3. Record country, preferred channel, source, permission status/evidence and owner. Qualification requires identifiable business, service fit, specific observed issue and a legitimate permitted contact path. Unknown permission means HOLD. A published WhatsApp number alone is insufficient; use opt-in or an inbound request. Check jurisdiction and platform requirements before approving any commercial message.
4. Review mobile/contact flow, preferably with SiteCheck or the existing saved-HTML reviewer. State observations, not guessed revenue loss. Select truthful labelled demos at https://www.xendersecrets.com/portfolio; verify the chosen live link. Store portfolio/demo/free-check/preview URL in the lead. Never claim demo projects are paid clients.
5. Draft one personal observation, a useful offer, sender identity and one clear question. Use the local currency/approved quote policy; no unverified discounts or delivery promises. For first touch prefer asking whether they want a free check. Approval records the exact message, recipient, channel and expiry.
6. Before sending, check suppression, latest reply, contact validity, consent/basis, channel preferences, previous touches and approval. No same-day multi-channel chasing. Platform limit is a ceiling, not permission. Pause on warnings, bounces, complaints or auth errors. Sending is human-reviewed or through an explicitly approved supported API.
7. Log touch ID, provider message/thread ID, sent-at time, exact copy hash, approval ID and result. Sent requires provider evidence; pending/unknown is not sent. Bounces mark bad_contact, stop follow-ups and research a verified replacement; do not silently change identity. Replies record classification (interested, question, not now, decline, opt-out) and next action. Opt-out suppresses the contact immediately across all channels; preserve minimal suppression evidence.
8. Follow-ups: at most two unanswered follow-ups, proposed day 3 and day 7 in the recipient's business hours, only where permission still permits. Each needs a new useful detail and approval. Decline/opt-out/reply cancels the sequence. Silence after final touch → CLOSED_NO_REPLY. Legacy Day 2/5/12 cadence is superseded for new outbound work.
9. Positive reply → discovery → scope/quote → approved payment terms → delivery task → mobile/functional acceptance → handover. Track proposal, won value, cash collected and invoice separately. Ask written permission before publishing client work or testimonials.

## Draft templates (fill with verified facts)

Email subject: A small website observation for {business}

Hi {name/team}, I noticed {specific verified issue} on {page}. I run Xender Secrets and build websites for {relevant business type}. Would a short free fix list be useful? Here is a labelled example: {relevant portfolio/demo URL}. If you prefer no further contact, reply “stop” and I will record that. {real sender name, business identity and jurisdiction-required contact/address details}.

LinkedIn, after a permitted connection: Hi {name}, your {specific business detail} caught my attention. I noticed {website observation}. Would a short fix list help? No attachment or unsolicited multi-message pitch. Keep conversations manual unless LinkedIn explicitly authorizes the integration.

WhatsApp, only opted-in/inbound: Hi {name}, Sahil from Xender Secrets here, following your request for {topic}. Here is {approved check/demo}. Would you prefer to continue here or by email? Reply stop anytime.

Follow-up: One additional detail for {business}: {new useful observation}. If useful, I can send {check}. If this is not a priority, I will close the loop after this follow-up.

Upwork/Freelancer: respond to a posted need using native proposal rules, honest portfolio, and a tailored scope. Do not circumvent off-platform communication/payment rules or buy proposal credits without approval. Shopify is a prospect segment/partner ecosystem, not a mass-contact directory.

## Country discovery targets (verify directory activity and terms before use)

| Market | Candidate sources | Initial research focus | Sending gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| USA | Google Maps/Business listings, Yelp, YellowPages.com | service SMBs in one city | review CAN-SPAM identity/address/unsubscribe requirements; no guessed details |
| Canada | YellowPages.ca, Yelp Canada, Canada411 | local service SMBs | consent evidence; conspicuous publication is conditional, not blanket permission |
| UAE/Dubai | Connect.ae, UAE Yellow Pages, Dubai business directories | clinics, restaurants, consultants | prior permission/default HOLD pending applicable local review |
| Australia | YellowPages.com.au, True Local; Hotfrog candidate | trades/services | consent evidence and compliant identification/unsubscribe |
| Philippines | BusinessList.ph, Yellow Pages, PhilippineCompanies | clinics/local services | prior permission/default HOLD pending local review |
| UK | Yell, Thomson Local, FreeIndex | incorporated service firms | distinguish corporate subscribers from sole traders/partnerships; uncertain → consent required |
| Germany | Gelbe Seiten, Das Örtliche, 11880 | service businesses with German-language reviewer | consent-first; no cold electronic marketing without reviewed basis |

Current conservative default is opt-in/permission for all countries until the campaign basis is documented. This is an operating gate, not a claim that all countries have identical laws. Directory existence, current limits and terms must be checked per campaign.

## Official policy references checked 2026-10-09

- [Canada CRTC guidance](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm): retain evidence for consent/basis, identification and unsubscribe.
- [UK ICO B2B guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/): corporate/individual subscriber distinctions; guidance is under review.
- [LinkedIn agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement): use permitted platform mechanisms; do not automate unauthorized scraping/DMs.
- [WhatsApp business policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy): recheck current opt-in and messaging requirements before account activation.
