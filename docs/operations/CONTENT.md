# Instagram content engine and community SOP

## Goal and measurement

Aim for 1,000,000 accounts reached in a rolling 30-day window, organic first. This is an aspirational target. Use account-level unique reach from native Insights; summing reel reach double-counts people and must be labelled gross reel reach. Never replace reach with views or promise a result. Capture baseline before setting growth expectations.

Weekly scorecard: account reach, views, average watch time, completion (where available), shares/reach, saves/reach, follows, profile visits and caption-link enquiries. Store metric definition, source, period and observed-at time. Missing analytics is UNKNOWN, not zero. No bot views, fake followers or engagement farms.

## Production pipeline

Idea → researched script → storyboard → draft asset → factual/rights review → exact asset/caption approval → scheduled manually/native API → provider post ID → 24h/72h/7d review. Start with five reels/week, two LinkedIn posts/week, two Facebook adaptations/week and two optional Telegram resources/week; adjust after two weeks of actual data. Quality and permissions override cadence. No publishing until account and copy are approved.

Mix: 30% original tech humour, 25% original serialized fiction, 20% practical tech demonstrations, 15% original cartoons, 10% lawful free AI workflows. Use original characters/voice/music or documented licenses. Clearly distinguish fiction from facts. No celebrity voice clones, unauthorized copyrighted characters, piracy, account abuse or paywall bypass. “Free AI” means official free tiers, local open models or legitimate promotions with eligibility and date checked at publication.

Xender promotion appears only in Instagram reel captions: never in spoken lines, visual watermark, end card or storyboard. Caption may end “Need a business website? Explore Xender Secrets — link in bio.” Keep hashtags relevant. LinkedIn/Facebook may present Xender openly but must retain truthful demo labels.

## One reel brief

Content ID, pillar, audience, hook, 15–35s script, shot-by-shot storyboard, caption, rights/source links, official free-tier proof if applicable, CTA, asset URL/hash, owner, reviewer, approval, scheduled timezone, provider post ID and metrics.

## Weekly experiment

Test one variable at a time: first-frame hook, length or storytelling ending. Use different original drafts, not duplicate spam. Compare at equal 72-hour age; report sample size and baseline. Choose the next experiment based on share/save/watch evidence; small samples are directional. Reuse winning structure with new content. Stop factual claims when the official offer changes.
