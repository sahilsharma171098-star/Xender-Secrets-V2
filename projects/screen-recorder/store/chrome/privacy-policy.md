# Xender Screen Recorder privacy policy

Updated 17 October 2026. Applies to the Chrome version 0.1.1.

When you click Start recording, Chrome asks you to choose a screen, window or tab. If selected, your microphone and webcam require separate browser permission. Available screen/tab sound can be included when you choose to share it. Availability depends on your OS and sharing source.

The extension processes screen video, shared audio, microphone voice, webcam images and the timestamp notes you enter solely to make your recording and its notes. Screen recordings may contain website content and personal information that you choose to show. Avoid recording private information you do not want in the saved file. Capture is started by you, not in the background.

Capture and processing happen locally. Videos and notes remain in this recorder tab's memory until downloaded, replaced by another completed recording, or the tab is closed. The extension stops at approximately 250 MiB of video chunks; a chunk and total memory use may be larger. Download before closing; reloads and crashes can lose unsaved work. Pausing pauses the saved recording, while microphone/camera streams remain active until Stop. Stop ends capture streams.

There is no account, analytics, advertising, cloud upload, third-party data sharing or data sale. We do not receive recordings, notes or device captures. User-initiated downloads are saved wherever Chrome or the user chooses; you control those files and can delete them using your device's file manager. The extension cannot delete downloads after saving.

Optional support links open the Xender Secrets website only when clicked. No video or note is attached to them; information you choose to submit there is subject to the website's privacy practices. Support: https://www.xendersecrets.com/contact .

Data is used only for these recording features. It is not used for unrelated advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or lending. Any later cloud feature would require an updated policy and separate informed consent.
