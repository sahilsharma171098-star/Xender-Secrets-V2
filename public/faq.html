Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about the shop, full-stack platform, projects, products, services, novels and policies.

SHOP & ECOMMERCE

Shop & Ecommerce

How do I order from the shop?

Add listed products to your cart, complete checkout and submit the order. The order is stored on the server and Xender Secrets can confirm fulfilment and payment with you.

How is payment handled?

Orders can be submitted with Cash on Delivery or payment-after-confirmation. A direct online payment gateway is not enabled yet.

Will products eventually connect to Amazon?

Products can also be linked to their official Amazon listing when an Amazon listing is available.

How is shipping calculated?

The current storefront applies free shipping at ₹499 or above and ₹49 below that threshold. Fulfilment details can be confirmed with the order.

WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT

Website Development

What is the difference between frontend, backend and full stack?

Frontend is the user-facing interface. Backend handles server logic and data. Full stack combines both into a connected application.

Can the website builds be customized for a client?

Yes. The portfolio builds can be customized with client branding, content, pages, workflows and integrations.

Do the full-stack builds use a real backend?

Yes. The platform uses Cloudflare Worker API routes with persistent SQLite-backed Durable Object storage for accounts, orders, enquiries, bookings and application data.

Can you add a database, login or payment system?

Database-backed accounts and server sessions are already implemented. Additional payment providers and project-specific integrations can be added based on requirements.

AI & DIGITAL PRODUCTS

AI & Digital Products

What are AI prompt packs?

They are structured prompts designed for defined tasks such as research, drafting or workflow support. They should be treated as tools, not guaranteed-outcome products.

What kinds of workflow templates can be built?

Examples include lead trackers, research workflows, content processes, reporting templates and repeatable operational checklists.

Does AI replace human review?

Not for every task. Important outputs should be checked, especially when customer, financial, legal or business-critical decisions are involved.

LEAD GENERATION & SALES

Lead Generation & Sales

Can Xender Secrets help build lead lists?

Lead research and structured prospect lists can be scoped using relevant public business information and a defined target customer profile.

Do you send spam messages to prospects?

No service should rely on indiscriminate spam. Outreach should be relevant, lawful and based on a legitimate business reason.

Can sales scripts be customized?

Yes. Scripts can be adapted to product, audience, channel, objections and the customer's real sales process.

NOVELS & READING

Novels & Reading

Are the novels hosted directly by Xender Secrets?

The completed classics library primarily links to original Project Gutenberg source pages rather than copying or reselling the full texts.

Why are some Chinese novels in Chinese only?

Availability varies by title and edition. The library links to public-domain source editions where available and avoids copying copyrighted modern translations.

Are Xender Originals the same as the classics library?

No. Public-domain classics are a curated reading library. Xender Originals is the brand’s original binge-style English web fiction, published separately from the classics library.

BUSINESS, CONTACT & POLICIES

Idea Catalog & Build Board

How does the Idea Catalog work?

Members can publish an app, website, automation or AI workflow idea in plain language. Other visitors can browse the idea and open its community discussion.

Can Xender build an idea from the catalog?

Yes. Use the Build this action on an idea to start a private scoping conversation. Public posting is optional; confidential ideas can be discussed privately.

Business, Contact & Policies

How can I contact Xender Secrets?

Use the Contact page, WhatsApp or the site chatbot. The website lists the current business contact details.

Where can I find privacy, terms and refund information?

Privacy, Terms and Refund pages are linked in the footer across the website.
