WHAT IT CHECKS

47 practical checks. Clear fixes.

Every issue explains what's wrong, why it matters and what to change. You get a Website Health Score from 0 to 100. It is a documented Xender SiteCheck heuristic score, not a Lighthouse score.

SEO

Search basics

Title, meta description, canonical, noindex, H1 and Open Graph previews for WhatsApp and LinkedIn.

ACCESSIBILITY

Usable by everyone

Alt text, form labels, button and link names, heading order, ARIA mistakes and measured text contrast.

USABILITY

Things that frustrate

Links that go nowhere, malformed URLs, forms without a submit button and missing autofill.

CONVERSION

Friction to enquiries

Vague or competing calls to action, no visible contact method, missing trust pages and long forms.

TECHNICAL

Security & basics

HTTPS, mixed content, insecure forms, mobile viewport, oversized images and verified broken links.

SiteCheck is a fast automated check, not a complete accessibility (WCAG) audit or a performance test. Conversion suggestions are clearly labelled as heuristics.

PRIVACY FIRST

Your pages stay in your browser.

SiteCheck has no servers and no accounts. It only runs when you click it.

  • Runs only when you click it, only on the current tab.
  • Analysis happens locally. Audit results are never sent to Xender Secrets.
  • Never reads form values, passwords, cookies or browsing history.
  • No analytics, no ads, no remote code.
  • Two permissions only: activeTab and scripting.
  • Read the SiteCheck privacy policy.
