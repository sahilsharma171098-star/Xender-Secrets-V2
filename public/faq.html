SHOP & ECOMMERCE

Shop & Ecommerce

Are the products on the shop real and available to buy?

The current shop is an ecommerce demo. Product names, sample prices and checkout are placeholders until confirmed inventory and marketplace listings are connected.

Does the current checkout charge money?

No. The present checkout is a demonstration flow. It does not process a real payment or persist a real customer order.

Will products eventually connect to Amazon?

The storefront is structured so confirmed products can later link to their official Amazon listing or another approved checkout method.

How is shipping calculated in the demo?

The demo shows a simple sample rule for interface testing. Final shipping terms will be based on the real fulfilment method and product listing.

WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT

Website Development

What is the difference between frontend, backend and full stack?

Frontend is the user-facing interface. Backend handles server logic and data. Full stack combines both into a connected application.

Can the demo websites be customized for a client?

Yes. The demos are portfolio concepts. Branding, content, pages, workflows and integrations can be scoped for a real project.

Do full-stack demos use a real backend?

Yes, the interactive demos call Cloudflare Worker API routes. They intentionally use sample or non-persistent demo data rather than pretending to be production systems.

Can you add a database, login or payment system?

Those can be part of a production scope. The exact implementation depends on the project, security requirements, hosting and payment provider.

AI & DIGITAL PRODUCTS

AI & Digital Products

What are AI prompt packs?

They are structured prompts designed for defined tasks such as research, drafting or workflow support. They should be treated as tools, not guaranteed-outcome products.

What kinds of workflow templates can be built?

Examples include lead trackers, research workflows, content processes, reporting templates and repeatable operational checklists.

Does AI replace human review?

Not for every task. Important outputs should be checked, especially when customer, financial, legal or business-critical decisions are involved.

LEAD GENERATION & SALES

Lead Generation & Sales

Can Xender Secrets help build lead lists?

Lead research and structured prospect lists can be scoped using relevant public business information and a defined target customer profile.

Do you send spam messages to prospects?

No service should rely on indiscriminate spam. Outreach should be relevant, lawful and based on a legitimate business reason.

Can sales scripts be customized?

Yes. Scripts can be adapted to product, audience, channel, objections and the customer's real sales process.

NOVELS & READING

Novels & Reading

Are the novels hosted directly by Xender Secrets?

The completed classics library primarily links to original Project Gutenberg source pages rather than copying or reselling the full texts.

Why are some Chinese novels in Chinese only?

Availability varies by title and edition. The library links to public-domain source editions where available and avoids copying copyrighted modern translations.

Are Xender Originals the same as the classics library?

No. Public-domain classics are a curated reading library. Xender Originals is reserved for future original fiction created for the brand.

Business, Contact & Policies
