Privacy policy · 15 October 2026

Files you select are processed locally in the extension. They are never uploaded to Xender Secrets or to a conversion service.

The extension does not collect or transmit personal data. It has no analytics, third-party advertising, trackers, accounts, remote scripts, cloud storage, or browsing-history access. It does not save file contents or file names in browser storage.

Selected files and generated results are held temporarily in the extension tab's memory. Clear files or close the tab to release them. Downloading an output saves a copy to the location you choose through the browser.

All conversion libraries, fonts, character maps and image decoders are bundled with the extension. They run locally, including when offline.

If you choose an external Xender Secrets website or support link, it opens an ordinary website tab. That website's own privacy policy applies. Your selected files and their names are not attached to these links.

Support: Xender Secrets contact page. Share only the information you choose in that website form; please do not send private documents.
