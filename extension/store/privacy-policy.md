# Xender SiteCheck — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Applies to Xender SiteCheck version 1.0.0 for Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox and Google Chrome._

Xender SiteCheck ("SiteCheck", "the extension") is a browser extension published by Xender Secrets (India). This policy explains exactly what the extension does with data. The short version: **SiteCheck analyzes the page you choose, inside your browser, and sends nothing to us.**

## What SiteCheck reads, and when
SiteCheck does nothing until you click its toolbar button. When you click it:

- It reads the **structure of the current tab's web page**: HTML elements and their attributes (for example titles, meta tags, headings, link addresses, image alt text, form field labels and types) and the computed styles needed to measure text contrast and element sizes.
- It reads the current tab's **address (URL)** to show the domain, decide whether the page can be analyzed, and resolve links.
- It runs only on that one tab, and only for as long as you are using it. It has no access to other tabs, and no access to any website you have not clicked it on.

## What SiteCheck never reads or collects
- The values you type into forms, including passwords, messages and payment details
- Cookies, local storage, session tokens or other authentication data
- Your browsing history, bookmarks or other tabs
- Your name, email address or any other personal information

## Where the analysis happens
All analysis is performed **locally in your browser**. Audit results are shown in the extension popup and are **not stored** after you close it. SiteCheck has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no remote code.

## The only network requests SiteCheck can make
- **Broken link check (optional).** If you click **Check links**, SiteCheck asks the website you are viewing whether up to 40 of its own links work. These requests go **only to that same website**, are sent without cookies, and their results stay in your browser. External links are never contacted. Nothing is sent to Xender Secrets.
- **Links you click.** If you click "Get a free website audit", your browser opens the Xender Secrets website like any normal link. SiteCheck does not send your audit results or the page you were viewing. Any information you then choose to share with Xender Secrets on its website is covered by the [Xender Secrets privacy policy](https://www.xendersecrets.com/privacy.html).

## Copy report
"Copy report" copies a text report to your clipboard only when you click it. What you do with it is up to you.

## Permissions
- `activeTab`: temporary access to the current tab, granted only when you click the SiteCheck button
- `scripting`: runs SiteCheck's built-in audit code in that tab

SiteCheck requests no host permissions, no storage and no background access.

## Your choices
You can choose not to click SiteCheck on any page, and you can remove the extension at any time from your browser's extensions page. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request, export or delete.

## Children
SiteCheck is a general web-development tool. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If a future version ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before release, and any new data use will require your explicit action.
