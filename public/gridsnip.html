Chrome extension · Version 1.0.0

Get tables out of images without retyping.

GridSnip OCR is designed to extract tabular content from images and paused videos and help you paste it into Excel or Google Sheets. Results depend on source image quality and table complexity—always review important numbers after extraction.

Who is it for?

Spreadsheets

Analysts & office teams

Move tabular information into a sheet for review and calculations instead of copying values manually.

Learning & research

Students & researchers

Capture a table visible in an image or lecture video and correct the extracted text before using it.

How to get started

  1. Open the official listing above and install the extension in desktop Chrome.
  2. Open an image or pause a video with a table, and follow the extension's capture instructions.
  3. Review the extracted rows and columns, then paste into Excel or Google Sheets.
