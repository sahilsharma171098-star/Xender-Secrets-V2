# Xender Screen Recorder

Summary: Record your screen with optional microphone, webcam and timestamp notes. Save locally.

## Description

Xender Screen Recorder helps you explain a bug, show a workflow or record a short tutorial. Choose a screen or window, optionally include your microphone and webcam, then download the recording to your device.

Features:
- Screen/window video with optional microphone audio.
- Optional circular webcam bubble.
- Pause and resume recording.
- Timestamp notes for bug reports and handoffs, downloaded as a text file.
- Local WebM video download, with no account or cloud upload.

Firefox desktop 142 or newer is required. System and tab audio are not captured in Firefox. Keep the recorder tab open, and keep it visible when using the webcam bubble. Videos are buffered in memory and stop at approximately 250 MiB of video data. Download before closing or recording again. A reload or crash can lose unsaved recordings.

No video editing, MP4 conversion, cloud sharing, transcription or subscription is included in this version.

## Privacy policy

Screen, microphone and webcam data are accessed only after you start recording and approve browser permissions. The video and timestamp notes remain in the recorder tab's memory until downloaded or the tab is closed. Downloads are saved to your device. Pausing pauses the saved recording; device streams remain active until Stop.

The extension does not upload recordings or notes, collect analytics, include advertising, sell data, or require an account. The optional external support website is subject to its own privacy practices. This policy describes version 0.1.1, dated 16 October 2026.
