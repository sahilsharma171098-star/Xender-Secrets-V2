What it does — and what it doesn't

We'd rather you know the limits up front.

  • Generates static websites: landing pages, brochure sites, portfolios, menus, simple multi-page sites.
  • Runs previews in an isolated sandbox with no access to your account.
  • Free tier runs on limited, shared free AI capacity — it can pause until 00:00 UTC on busy days.
  • No backends, databases, logins or payments are generated. Forms show a confirmation only, until connected.
  • No hosting on our side yet — you download the code, or ask us to launch it for you.
  • AI can make mistakes. Check text, images and claims before you publish.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What does Xender Builder generate?

Static websites made of HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript: landing pages, small business sites, portfolios and multi-page brochure sites. It does not generate server-side apps, databases or payments.

Is it free?

Yes. Guests get a few AI generations per day and free accounts get more. Generation runs on free AI capacity that is shared by everyone, so when today's capacity is used up it resumes at 00:00 UTC.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Download the ZIP and host it anywhere. You are responsible for checking the content, images and claims before publishing.

Can you put it live on my domain?

Yes. The Xender Secrets team can polish, launch and connect your generated site to your domain, real contact forms and WhatsApp. Request a quote.
